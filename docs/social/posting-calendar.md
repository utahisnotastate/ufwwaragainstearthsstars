# Posting Calendar

> **In one sentence:** What we post, when we post it, and who checks it first.

This calendar matches the [Disclosure Plan](../plan/disclosure-plan.md). All content lives in this repository first; social media posts are copies.

## Standard times

All times are **US Eastern**. UTC equivalents: add 5 hours until 13 March 2027, then add 4 hours (US daylight saving time starts 14 March 2027).

| Slot | Time (ET) | What |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday A | 10:00 | X Article (long form) |
| Tuesday B | 10:30 | X Thread (links to the article and dossier) |
| Wednesday A | 12:00 | Myth-vs-fact post |
| Wednesday B | 16:00 | Kid fact post (after school, for families to read together) |
| Thursday | 19:00 | Live office hour (45 min, captioned, recorded) |
| Friday | 12:00 | "Questions you asked" recap post (optional) |

## Who reviews what

| Role | Reviews |
|---|---|
| **Comms Lead** | Writes or edits all posts; final formatting and links |
| **Science reviewer** (Science Lead or a rotating maintainer who did not write the post) | Every science claim, number, date and evidence label |
| **Education Lead** | Kid fact posts, kid activities and any post aimed at families |
| **Moderation and Child-Safety Lead** | Any post that invites children's participation; office-hour plans |

No post goes out without the **science reviewer's** sign-off (two-person rule).

## Short video ideas

Each week has an optional 30–60 second video. Use simple props, no faces of children, captions always on.

## Live Q&A rules

- Captions on. Recording posted within a week.
- Questions from children are read aloud **without names** by the host.
- No private messages with minors, before, during or after.
- At least two adults from the project present.
- Start every session with a 30-second reminder: what "disclosure" means, and that we don't give health advice.
