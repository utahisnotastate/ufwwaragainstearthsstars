# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who loves the sky — children and adults, beginners and experts — regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, religion, or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and young people.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- Give credit to others' work and data.
- Admit mistakes openly; correct them gracefully.
- Treat professional scientists and agencies with respect, even when you disagree.

## What is not allowed

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — online or offline.
- Sharing anyone's private information, including any personal details of a child.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Claiming discoveries as "confirmed" without independent confirmation, or spreading information you know to be false.
- Encouraging others to target, pressure or harass scientists, agencies or volunteers.

## Child safety

Anyone under 18 should take part with a parent, guardian or teacher. Adults must never ask a young contributor to move to private messages or share personal information. Report any concern to the maintainers immediately.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the project maintainers through the contact listed on the repository page. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or ban participants who break these rules.
